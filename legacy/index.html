Trust the code.Not the team.

      

        
Trade-only agent
The desk's HyperCore agent can place and cancel orders. It has no withdraw path. A leaked key can't move funds.

        
Hard limits on-chain
Max position, max daily loss and max drawdown are enforced in the contract. Breach one and quoting stops.

        
Everything public
Every fill, every burn and every parameter change is an event. Changes go through a 48-hour timelock.

Questions.Answered.

      

        
Is the desk trading right now?
Not yet. The live desk needs Elysium's Phase 2 upgrade, which adds the HyperCore precompile and ElysiumCoreWriter. Until then the app shows a clearly labelled preview. The token and liquidity are live from launch.

        
Why Elysium?
Elysium is an EVM L2 for Hyperliquid with 100 ms blocks, HYPE as gas and, from Phase 2, native read and write access to HyperCore order books. A contract that makes markets on Hyperliquid isn't practical on any other chain.

        
Can the team take the treasury?
No. The trading agent can't withdraw, the 20% desk allocation is held by the contract, and parameters only change through a public 48-hour timelock.

        
What if the desk loses money?
Losses are capped per day and overall. When a limit is hit, quoting pauses automatically and the event is public. Burns only come from realized profit, never from principal.

        
How do I buy $MAKO?
Bridge HYPE to Elysium, open the app, connect your wallet and swap. The official contract address is posted only on makodesk.xyz and @makodesk.
